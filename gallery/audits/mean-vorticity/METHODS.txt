AVERAGED WAVE-PACKET VORTICITY BALANCE

This is read-only postprocessing of the seven completed Unity matched pairs.
The simulated velocity, initial vorticity, coherent current, and packet are
retained. There is no new simulation or imposed two-dimensional assumption.

With a transverse average over the entire periodic y direction, U=<u>, W=<w>,
and Q=<q>, where q=curl_y(uL-us) is Eulerian vorticity and uL advects it:
  B=U*Q_x+W*Q_z
  A=<u*q_x+w*q_z>
  E=A-B
  Rbar=<omegaE_x*v_x+q*v_y+omegaE_z*v_z-v*q_y>
  C=Rbar-E
The ideal mean equation is Q_t+B=C. Products are formed before averaging
when computing A and Rbar; products of the averaged velocities and vorticity
gradients define B. Signed averages are taken BEFORE spatial squares or norms.
Periodicity forces <v_y>=0 and mean in-plane incompressibility. It does not
force C=0. E, Rbar and their net C are reported separately to retain cancellation.

Two averages are distinguished. seed_N denotes the transverse mean within one
realization. coherent denotes transverse AND equal-weight seven-seed averaging
of primitive and product fields before forming B,E,C. There is no Bessel
correction: these are finite-average identities, not population estimators.
C_within_seed is the arithmetic average of the per-seed C fields. C_interseed
is coherent C minus C_within_seed, and equals minus the interseed covariance
Cov(U,Q_x)+Cov(W,Q_z). These fields are signed before regional norms.

Dense y_averages.jld2 records provide native U,V,W at nominal 0.1-s intervals.
At each 3-D snapshot time, three actual saved times are chosen to bracket it.
A quadratic interpolant estimates Q_t at the exact snapshot time. Temporal
strides 1 and 2 test approximately 0.1- and 0.2-s sampling. Packet Stokes fields
are recomputed at each saved time. There is no extrapolation or endpoint
derivative: the initial snapshot has temporal_stride=0 and no temporal ratios.
Q_interpolation_error compares interpolated dense Q with exact snapshot mean Q.

observed=Q_t+B measures the mean material vorticity tendency directly.
unclosed=observed-C retains temporal/spatial diagnostic errors, interpolation
effects and unresolved model/numerical tendencies. It is NOT a pure dissipation
measurement and is NOT assumed small. The geometric closure C is obtained
independently from the instantaneous 3-D snapshot terms.

packet_minus_control denotes the difference of already formed packet and
matched-control budgets. In particular delta B=Bpacket-Bcontrol, not advection
formed from delta velocity and delta Q alone. The paired coherent result
retains cross terms and avoids diluting the wave response with background Q.

Native staggered interpolation, Float32-rounded stretched z faces, native
continuity, and three-node diagnostic derivatives match the earlier full-field
audit. Spatial stencil spacings 1 and 2 are compared on identical coverage.
Regions: x=1..11 m; four vertical edge cells excluded; full interior, upper
Stokes layer and PIV window. Local packet-age bins are before, leading, core,
trailing, after and all. Actual cell thickness weights the spatial moments.
No regional or temporal averaging is performed before taking the signed y
and coherent means. Norms are RMS over the retained x,z cells of those means.

Ratios include observed/(||Q_t||+||B||), C/(||Q_t||+||B||),
unclosed/(||Q_t||+||B||+||C||), C/B and tau0*observed/Q. Compare absolute
norms and the paired response as well; a large background Q can dilute a rate,
and tiny advection can inflate an advection-normalized ratio. Undefined ratios
remain blank. These two spatial/temporal stencils do not establish simulation
convergence. Seven retained seeds do not support a significance or unbiased-
population claim. No closed budget is asserted unless the measured terms agree.

Source and campaign fingerprints are checked before/after reading. Native file
sizes and mtimes are checked; metadata is hashed. Private 2-D diagnostic maps
and per-seed tables remain on Unity. Only derived statistics, methods, figures
and completion receipts are published. The original completed campaign remains
unchanged.
