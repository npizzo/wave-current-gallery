The physical stress is centered/collocated from velocities; it is not the WENO numerical momentum flux.
Stress profiles include zero flux at impermeable top/bottom faces. Comparison uses the same center/face locations as the diagnostics.
The inferred O(amplitude^2) mean response neglects feedback of that mean change on the linear mode.
A ratio of mean-response RMS values does not verify the sign or depth shape of the observed mean.
Near stress zero crossings, small frequency errors can reverse the instantaneous stress sign; all negative-correlation samples are listed without a masking threshold.
Observed refinement is consistent with a leading spatial error but is not a full convergence theorem.
Stable-branch half-timestep refinement was not part of this campaign.
No claim about the nonlinear turbulent jet mechanism follows from these parallel-base modal comparisons.
