Deep-water current spectra to 1000 eddy times

Exponential Stokes profile · 4096 × 2048 · one matched seed pair · 5 November 2026

100 Te300 Te600 Te900 TeDirectional transferMethods & data

The upper-layer spectra become strongly anisotropic as the jets develop. At 900 Te, the off-axis energy fraction in the 16–24-cycle band is about 90–91% below the matched no-wave control in the upper 0.25 depth, with both estimators agreeing. The 8–16-cycle band is about 78% lower.

A curved cascade bottleneck is not yet established. The spectra show concentration near the jet axis, but the full-depth conservative nonlinear input is not consistently suppressed across bands and stages. Local-window budgets also contain substantial pressure and vertical-transport contributions. These are results from one seed pair, not an ensemble.

The experiment

The prescribed deep-water Stokes shear is ∂zus = 0.25e8z, with us = 0.03125e8z and surface-wave wavenumber k = 4. The domain is 0 ≤ x < 4, −2 ≤ z ≤ 0, so kH = 8. Both exactly two-dimensional runs use the same seed-1 initial ring at 27 cycles per original unit length, u0 = 1.563598513 × 10−4, and Te = 37.69911184. Evolution uses WENO9/RK3. One run has waves; its control has none. No constant-gradient run is used for this result.

Does nonlinear transfer establish a bottleneck?

The preselected test compares net conservative nonlinear input into fixed off-axis regions and a near-jet cone, |K|/κ ≤ 0.1. Positive input supplies energy; negative input removes it. The full-depth native calculation provides a conservative advection diagnostic without an artificial depth-window boundary. It includes the dynamically different lower layer, so it cannot by itself isolate an upper-layer barrier.

Resolution and interpretation

At D = 0.25 the direct vertical bin spacing is 1/D = 4 cycles; reflection gives a 2-cycle spacing by extending the data, not by adding independent observations. At D = 0.125 those spacings become 8 and 4. The Stokes gradient changes by e² ≈ 7.4 across the full D = 0.25 support, and by e ≈ 2.7 across D = 0.125. Thinner windows make the background more local while reducing vertical spectral resolution. The 1–4-cycle angular comparison is unresolvable in the upper-quarter window. The 4–8-cycle result is estimator sensitive. These limitations prevent a reliable fitted curved exclusion boundary here.

All spectra use the native-resolution fields. Velocity images alone are averaged over 4 × 4 cells for display; their vertical scale is expanded for readability, and colours clip outside ±4u₀. Local spectra collocate staggered velocities, apply the stated taper and divide by its mean square. Signed-K displays reconstruct conjugate partners from the stored positive-K half-plane; K > 0 multiplicity is undone before mirroring. The retained range is |K|/(2π), |ℓ|/(2π) ≤ 40. No fitted Rhines curve, zero padding or spectral smoothing is applied.

Validation and limits

All 2,002 online diagnostic files passed the stored conservation, pressure-projection and Parseval checks; the largest retained-horizontal Parseval discrepancy is 6.8 × 10−16. Independent SciPy transforms of the archived velocity fields check all three local depths, both estimators and all four stages. The two runs have identical initial velocity files. Finite-time budget checks use the 40-Tₑ energy change and the sampled instantaneous tendencies; they are not exact RK-step closure. Across the listed 1–24-cycle regions, the median relative residual is 0.18% for the full-depth budget and 0.13% for the upper-quarter Hann budget; maxima are 14.5% and 5.2%, respectively, including small-net-input regions. No grid-convergence or across-seed reproducibility claim follows from this one pair.
