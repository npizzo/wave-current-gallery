Window means of native full-depth kinetic-energy modes. Columns are K/(2pi), ell/(2pi), followed by constant-gradient (beta) and matched no-wave energies for 10–15, 15–20, 20–25 and 25–30 Te. Each mean contains three seeds and six snapshots at integer Te, including both endpoints; adjacent windows share one snapshot. Wavenumbers are cycles per original unit length. The file contains the displayed crop |K/(2pi)| <= 14 and 0 <= ell/(2pi) <= 14. Positive vertical modes store their full energy. For the mirrored figure, split each ell>0 energy equally between positive and negative ell, retaining ell=0 once. Normalize both cases in each row by their common maximum over the full retained spectrum (|K/(2pi)| <= 50, 0 <= ell/(2pi) <= 50) after splitting. The figure shows log10(E/Emax), clipped to [-3,0]. CSV values are linear energies, neither normalized nor clipped. Full time-resolved arrays and simulation fields remain archived on Unity.
