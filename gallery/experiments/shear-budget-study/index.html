What does the 3D term do to the jet?

The pilot linked Eulerian shear, three-dimensional growth and additional jet erosion. This study measures the direct signed contribution of the complete 3D feedback to the forced mean-vorticity and jet budgets, and adds targeted numerical checks.

Native forced vorticity budget: ∂t Q = A₀ + C₃D + W + D + F. A₀ is the native tendency from the transverse-mean velocity; C₃D is the difference between full-flow and mean-flow native advection curls. W, D and F retain wave, viscous and prescribed forcing terms. Pressure curl is checked independently. The complete C₃D includes transverse correlations, not only one stretching term. Added wind acts through the velocity boundary stress and is therefore included in D; F is the prescribed background-maintaining body force.

Direct budget and intervention answer different questions

Signed projections determine whether C₃D produces or removes mean-vorticity variance and jet-mode energy along the actual trajectory. The full 2D-versus-3D intervention also changes the later resolved eddies and therefore measures a broader response. We retain forcing and viscosity in both budgets, and report temporal closure before interpreting integrated terms. Complete transverse-mean enstrophy separates into horizontal-mean shear and x-varying contributions: Z(Q) = Z(⟨Q⟩ₓ) + Z(Q − ⟨Q⟩ₓ). The shear component and current modes address the jet; the x-varying component addresses its resolved eddies. Positive enstrophy production need not mean a stronger jet.

Legacy maps provide retrospective spatial budgets at their saved cadence. New one-second scalar moments improve temporal closure without interpolating the old data. The map integral and the scalar enstrophy integral have separate closure tests. Five percent is a numerical screening tolerance, not a physical transition criterion.

Matched interventions and numerical checks

The 200, 400 and 600 s windows are retained alongside later outcomes. A single final three-dimensional energy fraction or a rematched late extremum does not identify when the original jet was eroded.
