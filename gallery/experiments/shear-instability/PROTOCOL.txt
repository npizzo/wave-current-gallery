EULERIAN-SHEAR / LANGMUIR / JET-EROSION PILOT
Protocol fixed before the four new production simulations are submitted.

Question
Does the wind-driven evolution of Eulerian shear predict transverse roll growth and the extra erosion of an established wave-organized jet? The control's eventual viscous decay is not the target phenomenon. Small 3D feedback is a necessary-condition argument, not a promise that a jet persists indefinitely.

Existing numerics reused
Use the two completed full3D wind-onset branches (source a9775218799e02ea, no-wind and wind-on) from the same native jet-bearing parent at physical600s. Their added friction velocities are0 and2mm/s. Imported configurations, completion/backup receipts and every consumed diagnostic are hash-pinned in references-manifest.json; their original source, outputs and assessments remain unchanged. Historical results are exploratory reference evidence, not new independent validation.

Four new cases
1. Full3D, added u_star=0.75mm/s.
2. Full3D, added u_star=1.25mm/s.
3. Transversely independent2D3C, added u_star=0.
4. Transversely independent2D3C, added u_star=2mm/s.
All start from physical600s and run to1800s. Full3D branches import exactly the same native u,v,w arrays as the existing references. The2D3C branches start from their exact transverse means, retaining all three velocity components, the same horizontal-mean current and the same x-z eddies; only transverse dependence is removed. Their paired wind-minus-control response is compared with the corresponding full3D difference. Initial3D energy removed by projection is reported rather than called an identical full-state intervention.

Fixed settings
Lx=2m, Ly=1m, H=0.5m; grid128x64x96 for3D,128x1x96 for2D3C; waves travel alongx; k=6.1/m; u_s(0)=0.036649447248308646m/s; nu=3.3333333333333333e-6m2/s. Original maintained background and boundary gradients, wave profile, numerical scheme and maxdt0.02s are retained. Added wind is a constant top Lagrangian-momentum gradient increment u_star^2/nu, with depth-uniform return acceleration -u_star^2/H. The return has no curl and balances the additional bulk momentum input. This is an idealized maintained-background experiment, not an ocean mixed-layer parameterization. No new disturbance seed is introduced.

Why these forcing values
The inherited Eulerian surface shear is-0.335342442322/s and the Stokes shear is+0.447123256429/s. The added stress changes the first by u_star^2/nu. The0.75 and1.25mm/s cases bracket its sign reversal (about1.057mm/s for this fixed numerical viscosity). That boundary sign change is NOT declared a global Langmuir or jet-destruction threshold. The destabilizing layer must have finite depth and overcome dissipation; existing deep signed shear must also be retained in the profile analysis.

Predictors and checks
Q(z,t)=dUE/dz, beta_eff=dQ/dz, and Q*du_s/dz distinguish local roll destabilization from vortex-wave restoration. A constant-coefficient inviscid roll estimate has sigma^2=Q*du_s/dz * ky^2/(ky^2+kz^2); it is not used as an exact global growth rate. Solve the bounded viscous streamwise-independent profile problem, with homogeneous perturbation stress and impermeable boundaries, for transverse modes permitted byLy. Use the maintained laminar mean evolving from the common initial profile and prescribed stress as the prospective predictor, without fitting to the resulting turbulence. Applying the same calculation to measured profiles is a separate retrospective diagnosis. Report basis and sampled-mode convergence, identify unresolved maxima, and label integrated frozen-profile eigenvalue growth as an approximation to time-dependent amplification, not an exact propagator or guaranteed onset time. Nonmodal amplification and rapidly changing eigenvectors are not ruled out by stable instantaneous eigenvalues. Report cells per transverse wavelength and positive-shear-layer thickness in vertical cells. A continuum fastest mode below four cells per wavelength or a destabilizing layer below four vertical cells is not a resolved nonlinear prediction. Also report fastest growth restricted to at least four and six transverse cells per wavelength on the parent64-cell transverse grid; the2D controls use that same counterfactual susceptibility grid. Existingstrongwind data indicate that the continuum fastest mode may be close to the native grid limit. This pilot does not resolve that limitation by assertion; exact fastest-scale or onset-rate validation remains unresolved without later grid refinement.

Primary comparisons
The first600s after switching are primary;1200s checks persistence and later reorganization. Compare retained initial-jet prominence, depth and width at200,400,600s; report half-prominence loss only when sustained200s and resolved by at least four vertical cells. Track absolute3D androll amplitudes together with energy fractions and native complete3D feedback. Preselect3D-energy10% as a descriptive onset marker and publish the full traces; it is not a closure threshold. Compare the stress trend across0,0.75,1.25,2mm/s. The2D/3D difference-in-differences is [P3D,wind-P3D,control]-[P2D,wind-P2D,control], using the same initial prominence normalization. Earlier loss withwind in2D would demonstrate a route that does not require transverse motion; additional loss in3D measures the net consequence of allowing that motion, not a unique attribution to one isolated term. Resolve Eulerian mean-shear production and advective momentum redistribution where saved diagnostics support it; distinguish them from the imposed laminar profile response.

Interpretation
Assess three separate links: (A) destabilizing shear and predicted growth precede measuredroll/3D growth; (B) wind causes erosion beyond its laminar reference, establishing a nonlaminar contribution; (C) the wind-minus-control erosion contrast is more negative in3D than2D3C, establishing the additional consequence of allowing transverse motion. LinkB alone does not establish linkC. A mismatch is reported, not repaired by retuning the four-case pilot. A stable or unresolved local/profile predictor is not by itself a proof against every3D instability. Outcomes are descriptive support, mismatch or unresolved for each separate link. No universal parameter boundary, necessary-and-sufficient jet criterion, grid convergence of the nonlinear response, or ensemble significance is claimed from this small pilot. Later parameter sweeps are not authorized by this pilot setup.
