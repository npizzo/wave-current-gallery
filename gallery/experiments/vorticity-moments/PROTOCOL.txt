VORTICITY MOMENTS AND THREE-DIMENSIONAL FEEDBACK

Question
Can identifiable vorticity-production and transverse-variance terms diagnose when a y-averaged flow departs from its matched autonomous two-dimensional evolution? The principal comparison is a measured error in the mean current and vorticity, accompanied by the terms in their equations. A small geometric three-dimensional energy fraction alone is not treated as a closure criterion.

Physical configuration
All runs use incompressible wave-averaged dynamics in a horizontally periodic box, with impermeable free-slip upper and lower boundaries. The prescribed, steady, deep-water monochromatic Stokes velocity is along x: us_ref=S exp(2 k0 z), S=0.17^2 sqrt(9.81/6.1), k0=6.1 m^-1, z in [-0.5,0]. Actual Stokes velocity is WAVES*us_ref. The initial Eulerian mean is UE0=-alpha us_ref, and the initial Lagrangian mean is UL0=(WAVES-alpha)us_ref. Thus q0=-alpha us_ref_prime is retained explicitly, also in wave-off controls. It is not set to zero. A compensating force cancels the native viscous decay of UL0; it does not maintain eddy energy or prescribe the evolving mean current. There is no wind stress, density stratification, planetary rotation, wave packet, or horizontal inhomogeneity in this campaign. These are idealized mechanism tests, not direct repetitions of the Ellingsen laboratory experiment.

Definitions and equations
Write the Lagrangian velocity as (u,v,w), the Eulerian transverse vorticity as q=u_z-w_x-us_z, its fixed initial background as q0, and its anomaly as a=q-q0. For the present steady us(z), the continuum equation is D_L q=T+Dq, where
  T=q v_y+w_y v_x-u_y v_z.
Dq contains viscous, imposed-force, and any numerical contributions as individually identified by the native tendency diagnostics. The anomaly equation contains the additional background-advection term -w q0_prime, present even in exact two-dimensional motion. In the ideal continuum interior the prescribed viscous-background compensation yields Dq=nu Laplacian(q-q0); the analysis retains actual native viscous and force tendencies instead of assuming this identity at boundaries.

The volume q-enstrophy is <q^2>/2; its continuum material production is <qT> plus <qDq>, with advective boundary transport retained where applicable. The anomaly enstrophy <a^2>/2 additionally has production -<a w q0_prime>. Signed and absolute products are reported because a small signed global integral can result from cancellation rather than a small local term. The three constituents of T are saved separately.

Let Q=<q>_y, q'=q-Q, (U,W)=<(u,w)>_y, and F=(<u'q'>_y,<w'q'>_y). The continuum mean equation has C=<T>_y-div_xz F as its three-dimensional correction. Mean and transverse-variance enstrophy exchange enstrophy through F dot grad Q with opposite signs; the variance equation contains -F dot grad Q+<q'T>_y. Flux divergences and vertical boundary transport must be retained in local or depth-limited budgets. Production, transport, mean-variance exchange, and mean feedback are distinct quantities.

A genuinely y-independent field has T=0, including a two-dimensional three-component velocity v(x,z) that is not zero. Cancellation between the unsimplified tilting terms is essential. The converse does not hold: T=0 or <qT>=0 is not sufficient to establish two-dimensionality. Scalar enstrophy production also need not measure the full vector vortex-stretching process.

Numerical moments are formed with explicitly documented grid locations and quadrature weights. Native q tendencies are obtained by taking the same discrete curl of the actual momentum tendencies. Their moment contractions are algebraic chain-rule checks, independent of a continuum product-rule rearrangement. The latter has a spatial discretization residual. Temporal integration of sampled rates has a separate quadrature error. All three are displayed separately; no coarse-grid continuum residual is silently assigned to three-dimensional physics.

Experiment matrix: 36 runs, all 1200 s
- 15 central runs: alpha=.75; reference Re_lambda=100,200,300; initial y-dependent perturbation energy / y-independent in-plane eddy energy ratio eta=0,1e-8,1e-6,.01,1.
- 6 background-vorticity checks: alpha=.5 and 1, Re_lambda=300, eta=0,1e-6,1.
- 6 wave-off controls: alpha=.75, Re_lambda=100 and 300, eta=0,1e-6,1. Initial Eulerian q0 is held fixed between the matched wave-on and wave-off settings.
- 2 exact 2D3C nulls: alpha=.75, Re_lambda=100, eta=0, transverse velocity RMS .004 m/s, with waves on and off. All y derivatives vanish; v itself need not vanish.
- 3 spatial-resolution checks: central Re_lambda=300 triad eta=0,1e-6,1 on a 256x128x192 grid.
- 1 timestep check: central finite-disturbance Re_lambda=300 run at half the primary maximum step.
- 3 second-seed checks: central Re_lambda=300 triad eta=0,1e-6,1.

The primary grid is 128x64x96 over (Lx,Ly,H)=(2,1,.5) m. Initial in-plane eddy RMS is .004 m/s and injection wavelength is .25 m. Reference Re_lambda=U_2D_initial*lambda/nu sets nu=1e-5,5e-6,3.3333333333333333e-6 m^2/s. The tiny disturbances share their shape and differ by a factor 100 in initial energy; finite eta adds energy rather than replacing base-flow energy. The common initial q0 and in-plane eddy field, not total kinetic energy, define a matched comparison. The maximum step is .02 s on the primary grid, .01 s on the fine grid and explicit half-step case, additionally limited by the driver's advective and viscous restrictions. Spatial refinement is therefore accompanied by a smaller step; the separate half-step comparison checks temporal sensitivity independently for the finite case.

Outputs and storage
Global moments and height profiles are sampled every 1 s. Existing momentum diagnostics are also sampled every 1 s. Mean maps and mean-plane velocity snapshots are sampled every 20 s. Native three-dimensional velocity snapshots are written only initially and finally. Periodic restart checkpoints are transient and the final checkpoint is removed after successful completion. The persistent archive contains every completed run file, with hashes checked after streaming archive creation. Compact global statistics, sampled mean profiles, closure summaries, and plots are public; native snapshots and full 1 s height profiles remain private. Estimated uncompressed production footprint is 15-20 GiB; actual retained bytes are recorded. Runtime and package depots are reused from the pinned existing installation rather than copied.

Validation required before production
Validation uses the identical frozen source and environment. It runs manufactured identities and production tests, exact 2D and 2D3C nulls, CPU/GPU comparisons, restart comparisons, tiny-amplitude collapse, timestep refinement, and short primary/refined-grid integrations. Exact native decomposition and moment identities are checked separately from sampled-in-time closure. Nonzero v and vanishing T are required in the 2D3C null. A native-to-continuum production residual is measured on both primary and refined grids; its value is evidence about diagnostic resolution, not a pass/fail claim of physical two-dimensionalization. Long-run numerical sensitivity, second-seed consistency, and finite-time stability remain production outcomes.

Execution and publication
Production submission requires validation/passed.json for the current frozen source. At most two GPU array tasks run simultaneously. Existing run directories and completed results are never overwritten. Each completed simulation is verified and archived to persistent storage before an atomic completion receipt allows public analysis. Publishing is attached after every array member, once immediately after submission, and after the entire array. The existing gallery-wide lock serializes commits. The new page is experiments/vorticity-moments; previous packet, mechanism, and regime pages remain intact. A failed integration or failed backup is reported as failed, not counted as a completed scientific result. A resumed or extended campaign requires an explicit plan; this package does not silently rerun partial results.

Interpretation
Compare native three-dimensional mean feedback, moment production, transverse-variance growth, and mean-current error jointly. Large cancelling production terms are different from small terms. Anomaly production due to q0_prime must not be called a three-dimensional effect. Early linear disturbance growth, late nonlinear saturation, and numerical dissipation must be distinguished. Single-seed cells do not establish statistical significance or a universal transition. The Taylor-Proudman analogy motivates diagnostics but is not assumed as a theorem for variable Stokes shear, bounded depth, viscosity, or the transient Ellingsen packet.
