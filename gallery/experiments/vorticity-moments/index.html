Does 3D motion produce q-enstrophy?

Track the stretching and tilting terms that vanish for every transverse-independent flow. Compare signed production with its absolute local magnitude: cancellation is different from absence.

What grows by rearranging q₀?

A nonzero background-vorticity gradient can generate anomaly variance even in two dimensions. Keep this conversion separate from true 3D production and exchange between the mean and fluctuations.

Does the feedback change the jets?

Compare Eulerian mean currents and their changes for exactly 2D, tiny, intermediate and finite initial disturbances. Measure the signed 3D supply to the mean-anomaly budget alongside those profiles.

The scalar law and the 2D3C null test

Here q = ∂zuL − ∂xw − u′s is the Eulerian transverse component of vorticity. Its equation is DLq = T + Dq, where T = q∂yv + (∂yw)(∂xv) − (∂yu)(∂zv). Dq retains viscosity and the prescribed background-maintaining force. The derivative DL uses the Lagrangian velocity.

Thus Zq = ⟨q²⟩/2 measures one component of enstrophy, not the full vector enstrophy. T vanishes when ∂y=0, including in a 2D3C flow with nonzero v(x,z). Other vorticity components can still be stretched in that flow. The 2D3C cases test precisely this distinction.

Transverse periodicity makes ⟨∂yv⟩ vanish; it does not force ⟨q²∂yv⟩ or the tilting products to vanish. Likewise, −v∂yq is transport, not an independent enstrophy-production mechanism.

Background conversion and mean–variance exchange

The Eulerian background is UE0=−αus,ref+Uoffset, with q0=−αu′s,ref. It is not set to zero. In no-wave controls the actual Stokes drift vanishes while this prescribed Eulerian background remains. The native discrete-center q0 and analytic profile are both exported.

For a=q−q0, DLa=T−wq′0+Dq. Its variance can therefore grow through −⟨awq′0⟩ even when T=0. That background conversion is plotted separately from ⟨aT⟩.

For the transverse mean Q and fluctuation q′=q−Q, Zq=Zmean+Zvariance. With F=(meany(u′q′), meany(w′q′)), the exchange ⟨F·∇Q⟩ enters those two budgets with opposite signs. The production ⟨QT⟩+⟨q′T⟩ changes their sum. Zvariance/Za removes the large imposed background from the denominator, but is not used as a universal regime threshold.

Two independent residuals, including layer transport

The temporal check compares each observed moment change with the trapezoidal time integral of its freshly evaluated native rate. The native q tendency is multiplied by the full q field before averaging; it is not reconstructed from mean vorticity alone.

The spatial check compares that native rate with the continuum production, viscosity, maintenance forcing and transport terms. Discrete derivatives, interpolations and product-rule differences can leave a residual. Both signed residuals are shown explicitly; neither is adjusted to force closure or called pure physical dissipation.

Full-depth and upper Stokes-layer budgets are separate. The layer consists of complete grid cells with centers between −1/(2k) and the surface. Flux through its actual lower face is included. A layer budget without that transport would confuse motion across its boundary with production.

Experimental design and limits

The central background α=0.75 is tested at initial reference Reynolds numbers 100, 200 and 300 with η=0, 10⁻⁸, 10⁻⁶, 0.01 and 1. Additional background, no-wave, 2D3C, finer-grid, half-time-step and second-seed checks test the interpretation. Each run lasts 1200 s. η is the initial transverse-dependent perturbation energy relative to the prescribed 2D eddies; finite disturbances add energy.

All reported currents are Eulerian: UE=UL−us. Profile-change comparisons subtract each run’s own initial horizontal mean. Shared initial fields, settings and output checksums are verified before publication.

This finite-time suite can test production mechanisms and the usefulness of a mean description. It does not alone prove an asymptotic stability boundary, a universal rotation analogue, or a tracer-mixing barrier. The previous regime map and Ellingsen packet comparison remain distinct datasets; their results are not relabelled as outcomes of these new tests.
