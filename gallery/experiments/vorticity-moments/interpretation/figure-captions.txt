Parameter summary. All endpoint values are at 1200 s. Top panels use alpha=0.75, seed1; nonzero initial disturbance amplitude is plotted against the fixed surface Stokes speed. Exact 2D cases have zero 3D energy and zero paired-current error and are omitted from logarithmic axes. Reinj is varied through viscosity at fixed initial base eddy RMS, injection wavelength and wave profile. Fine-grid symbols also use half the maximum timestep; second-seed symbols are independent initial fields. Bottom panels separately change alpha, or remove waves while preserving the initial Eulerian background. Connecting lines guide the eye and do not define stability boundaries. No wind-stress axis was sampled.

History comparison. Both cases have Reinj=300 and alpha=0.75. The current-error history uses the depth RMS mismatch between changes from each run’s own initial mean, divided by the matched 2D final changed-current RMS. That denominator is fixed throughout each panel. The two curves describe different quantities; neither a final kinetic fraction nor a single threshold diagnoses autonomous 2D mean dynamics.
