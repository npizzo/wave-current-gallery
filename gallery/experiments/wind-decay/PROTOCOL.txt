WIND SHUTOFF AFTER LANGMUIR TURBULENCE: A MATCHED DECAY EXPERIMENT

Question
Can turbulence generated by sustained wind and surface waves organize into depth-dependent along-wave jets after the wind stops? Compare wind shutoff and continued wind from exactly the same developed turbulent state. This is an exploratory matched pair, not a statistical phase diagram or a convergence study.

Candidate selected before the decay calculation
Use the completed weak3d-u002-a075-s1 parent from the frozen wind campaign d6423e57b0ebb86f. Wind stress was imposed from t=0 with water-side friction velocity u_star=0.002 m/s. Initial transverse-dependent disturbance energy was 1e-6 times the base in-plane eddy energy. Surface Stokes speed is 0.036649447248308646 m/s with k=6.1 m^-1. The initial interior Eulerian current is -0.75 times the reference Stokes profile; this background has evolved freely and is not reset when branching.

Select the saved native state at t=1000 s, when total eddy RMS is about 5.39 mm/s and the x-invariant transverse overturning RMS is about 4.73 mm/s. Rolls account for approximately 77% of eddy kinetic energy. This state contains established Langmuir-roll geometry and a smaller surviving transverse-mean eddy component. At t=1200 s the parent already has considerably less roll organization, so the earlier state more directly tests decay from the developed-roll phase. Candidate selection uses only existing forced history, before either new result is known. Exact measurements and input hashes are supplied in reference/parent-selection.json and reference/parent-history.csv.

Matched interventions
Both branches load identical native u,v,w arrays at t=1000 s, verified against the original snapshot, metadata and source fingerprints. The wind-on branch continues u_star=0.002 m/s. The wind-off branch sets the imposed surface momentum stress to zero at that instant. Both retain the same prescribed monochromatic waves throughout. No velocity, current, vorticity, eddy spectrum or turbulent amplitude is reset.

The prescribed surface boundary condition is nu*partial_z(UL)=u_star^2, with zero bottom stress. The inherited model uses constant viscosity acting on prognostic Lagrangian velocity. Its uniform momentum-return force is -u_star^2/H, has zero curl, and is set to zero together with the wind stress in the off branch. Thus both branches preserve bulk momentum. The abrupt stress change can cause an initial boundary adjustment; it is part of the intervention and will be displayed. This is not a model of simultaneous wind and wave decay.

Numerics and duration
The identical box, resolution and numerical scheme are retained: Lx=2 m, Ly=1 m, H=0.5 m, grid 128x64x96, nu=1e-5 m^2/s, Centered2 advection, RK3 and adaptive advective/diffusive limits with maximum dt=0.02 s. No planetary rotation, buoyancy forcing or stratification is added. Integrate both branches from t=1000 to 4000 s, giving 3000 s after the switch. Scalar/profile diagnostics are recorded every 1 s and mean maps every 10 s. Native snapshots are retained at 1000,1100,1200,1400,1600,2000,3000,4000 s.

The first 600 s after the switch is the primary decay interval; the remaining 2400 s tests persistence and slower large-scale organization. For the retained viscosity, approximate cosine-mode amplitude damping times are 2533,633 and158 s for vertical modes 1, 2 and 4. Therefore the analysis must show early evolution and never judge formation solely from the final state after viscous decay.

Comparisons
Show the actual Eulerian mean-current profile and its depth-time evolution in both branches, differences from their common initial profile, and the matched off-minus-on difference. Show the original forced spin-up history before the split. Track total eddy motion, y-invariant eddy motion, the y-dependent energy fraction and x-invariant roll energy separately. Decreasing 3D energy alone does not establish the autonomous vorticity equation.

Measure the complete native transverse-mean 3D correction, its dimensional RMS and its accumulated magnitude over fixed 200 s windows normalized by the starting x-varying mean-vorticity RMS. Use the same physical boundary exclusion of two primary-grid cells and active Stokes-layer diagnostic as the preceding campaign. A small accumulated correction is descriptive evidence about applicability; this pair does not include independent two-dimensional forecast integrations.

Track full-profile interior extrema, their prominence, widths, persistence and vertical current modes. Use a prominence floor of 1% of the initial base eddy RMS (0.04 mm/s), presence in at least 80% of samples in a 200 s window, and flag widths below four vertical cells. Compare these against the same diagnostics at the switch, and plot changes across every window rather than only the final 200 s. A current maximum already present at the switch is inherited structure, not newly formed. A maximum in the difference profile alone is not enough to identify a new jet.

Distinguish turbulent organization from the mean shear's ordinary adjustment using a one-dimensional mean-diffusion reference starting with the same initial UL profile, the same discrete constant-viscosity operator, surface stress and uniform return force. Keep raw current profiles visible. This reference omits turbulent transport and is a diagnostic baseline, not another full-flow simulation. Record native advective momentum transfer into current bands and the separate viscosity, wave and forcing contributions. Neither a changing mean profile nor a candidate Rhines scale alone verifies the proposed beta-mediated mechanism or an inverse cascade.

Interpretation
A useful candidate sequence would show established roll turbulence at the switch, decay of rolls and 3D feedback after shutoff, and subsequently new or strengthened persistent interior current bands beyond the inherited/diffusive profile, with turbulent momentum transfer supporting their organization. The sustained-wind branch provides the matched counterfactual. If comparable structure appears in both, or is explained by mean diffusion/boundary adjustment, do not assign it uniquely to the decay mechanism. If no bands appear, report absence within this candidate and observation interval; a single pair does not exclude other regimes.
