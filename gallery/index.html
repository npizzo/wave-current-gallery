The physical setup

One packet. One matched control.

A nearly monochromatic packet crosses a bounded tank containing preconditioned turbulence. The comparison uses the Eulerian mean-velocity change before and after the packet, with the original sampling windows.

The new diagnostics separate the repeatable packet response from turbulent fluctuations, and distinguish momentum transport by motions that vary across the tank from motions that do not.
