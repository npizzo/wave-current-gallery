WAVE-PACKET VORTICITY AUDIT

Purpose
Measure the geometrical corrections to the proposed in-plane scalar-vorticity
equation from existing Unity snapshots. The simulation source, initial data,
timesteps, and completed analysis remain unchanged. This is CPU postprocessing.

Fields and exact continuum identity
All velocities are the full resolved Lagrangian-mean velocity (u,v,w), in the
current-following frame. We retain the coherent current, horizontal means and
all transverse modes. We do not subtract the packet-only flow or the ensemble
mean. Only the prescribed Stokes velocity is subtracted when computing Eulerian
vorticity omegaE=curl(uL-us). Stokes velocity is evaluated at native staggered
nodes and averaged with the same adjacent-face interpolation as the velocity.

q=omegaE_y=partial_z(u-us)-partial_x(w-ws).
For ideal, incompressible, unforced CL dynamics:
  (partial_t+u partial_x+w partial_z)q = R3D,
  R3D = omegaE_x*v_x + q*v_y + omegaE_z*v_z - v*q_y.
For this spanwise-uniform imposed packet, the alternative expression is
  R3D = w_y*v_x - u_y*v_z + q*v_y - v*q_y.
We check the two expressions algebraically, and measure the square of their
NET sum as well as separate norms. Cancellation is retained, not discarded.

Native continuity
Native adjacent-face differences give u_x, v_y, w_z at common cell centres.
Incompressibility requires v_y=-(u_x+w_z), so dividing their norms is not useful.
The relevant ratio is ||v_y||/sqrt(||u_x||^2+||w_z||^2); the report also gives
the equivalent in-plane-divergence form and the full-divergence error.
Native full-grid maximum divergence uses the same scaled tolerance (2e-4)
as the original campaign verifier. This is a software/numerical QC threshold,
not a physical criterion for two-dimensional dynamics.

Derivative evaluation and coverage
Snapshot native shapes are u:(Nx+1,Ny,Nz), v:(Nx,Ny,Nz), w:(Nx,Ny,Nz+1).
Velocities are averaged to cell centres for curls and nonlinear products.
Diagnostic x,z derivatives are quadratic-exact three-node formulas; z uses
the original stretched coordinates rounded to Float32. y is periodic. There
is no wrapping in x or z. Two derivative stencil spacings (1 and 2 cells) are
evaluated on the same data; native divergence is unchanged between them.
Four cells at each vertical boundary are excluded from ALL reported regional
and profile moments, including native-gradient moments, for a common coverage
that avoids one-sided/nested derivative stencils. Full-grid divergence maxima
are separately retained. This excludes about 8 mm immediately below the surface
at M2. Exact sampled depth bounds and volumes are in the tables. Native gradient
ratios and centred-gradient ratios are reported separately.

Regions: x between 1 and 11 m, with full vertical interior; upper Stokes depth
z>=-1/(2k); and PIV window -1.2<=kz<=-0.1. All are intersected with the common
vertical interior above. We weight by actual cell thickness, not by index.
Profiles retain each sampled depth. Local packet age is
  (t-(x-x0)/cg)/tau0.
Bins are before (<-3), leading [-3,-1), core [-1,1), trailing [1,3), after (>=3),
plus the all-ages bin. The no-wave control uses the same virtual packet clock.
No averaging across different saved times or packet-age bins is used to make
an ensemble result. These are spatial bins within sparse instantaneous snapshots,
not a densely sampled Lagrangian packet-following time history.

Packet sources
For a moving packet s=partial_x(ws)-partial_z(us) depends on x,z,t. We therefore
retain analytic s_t, u*s_x and w*s_z, their net s_material, and s*v_y. The supplied
s and derivatives are analytic cell-centre reference quantities; q itself uses
the discrete curl of the interpolated Eulerian velocity. A small discrepancy
between their discrete/analytic forms is possible. Stencil sensitivity and
manufactured tests assess diagnostic errors, not simulation convergence.
eta=curl_y(uL) has ideal RHS R3D-s_material. The manuscript convention
zeta=w_x-u_z=-eta has the opposite sign. A comparison with w*s_z alone would
omit time dependence and horizontal variation of the packet envelope.

Ratios and limitations
Absolute signed means, mean squares, RMS norms and maxima are retained.
Ensemble mean squares are averaged equally over the seven retained paired seeds
before forming norms/ratios. We do not average seedwise ratios.
  epsilon_div: in-plane divergence / uncancelled diagonal-gradient norm.
  R3D/(||u*q_x||+||w*q_z||): correction relative to in-plane advection;
      this denominator is reference-frame dependent.
  tau0*||R3D||/||q||: instantaneous correction rate on the packet-width timescale;
      it is not an integrated material change over tau0.
  cancellation: ||R3D|| divided by the sum of its four component norms.
Zero denominators are undefined. No noise floor is inferred automatically.
Seven upstream-retained seeds are not a convergence or unbiased-population test.

A small R3D would bound the ideal geometrical correction to scalar-q advection.
It would not, by itself, prove a Taylor-Proudman balance or material conservation.
Sparse saved snapshots do not resolve the temporal vorticity budget, and implicit
WENO dissipation is not measured here. A small net correction can arise through
cancellation of large three-dimensional terms without geometric columnarity.
No tracer diffusivity, energy-flux budget, or transport-barrier claim is made.

Outputs and provenance
Each seed writes regions.csv, profiles.csv, qc.csv and a completion receipt in
the separate gallery .unity-publisher audit directory. Snapshot files are read
with IOStream; their size/mtime are checked for changes and metadata is hashed.
Full snapshot bytes are not newly hashed. Campaign source fingerprints and
completed-run receipts are checked. Frozen audit code is hashed at submission.
Only small aggregate tables, figures, definitions and provenance are published
to gallery/audits/vorticity; native fields and large per-seed profiles stay on Unity.
